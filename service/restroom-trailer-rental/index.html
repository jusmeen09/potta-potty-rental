When Restroom Trailer makes sense

Restroom trailers provide a more finished interior and may include climate control, lighting, sinks, mirrors, and separate restroom stalls depending on the model. Compare trailers with standard and ADA units before choosing.

Star helps you make a rental inquiry to a local provider. The provider confirms the model, inventory, delivery, servicing, and final quote for your address.

Rental support for sites, events, and temporary facilities

Choose equipment around the people using it, the length of the rental, site conditions, and the service access available.

  • Weddings

    Confirm power and water at the venue early, since many trailers need both.

  • Corporate events

    Ask for a layout that matches guest count and event length, and where the trailer will park.

  • Film and production sites

    Check access for towing and setup, and whether the trailer can stay through the shoot.

  • VIP and hospitality areas

    Place the trailer close to the guests it serves, with lighting on the approach after dark.

Restroom trailer rental cost

Published restroom trailer prices run from about $300–$2,500+ per day and about $900–$3,000 for a weekend. The spread is wide because trailers range from compact units to large multi-stall models.

Stall count, climate control, the number of days, delivery distance, and whether the vendor supplies water and power move the price most. These are third-party figures, not Star rates.

Restroom Trailer Rental FAQs

Clear answers for planning quantities, placement, service, delivery, and pricing.

Published prices run about $300–$2,500+ per day in a Northeast vendor’s guide and about $900–$3,000 per weekend in Fixr’s national guide. Trailer size, dates, delivery distance, utilities, and service set the actual quote, which the vendor confirms.

Many trailers require a dedicated electrical connection and water source or supplied water option. Requirements vary by model, so utilities must be confirmed before delivery.

A firm, level placement area with adequate towing, turning, setup, and service clearance is typically required. Share photos and access details when available.

Many models offer heating or air conditioning, but features vary. Ask which trailer layouts and comfort features are available for your ZIP code and date.

Popular event dates and larger trailer sizes can book early. Call as soon as the venue, date, and approximate attendance are known.
