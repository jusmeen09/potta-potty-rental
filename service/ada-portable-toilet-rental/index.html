Rental support for sites, events, and temporary facilities

Choose equipment around the people using it, the length of the rental, site conditions, and the service access available.

  • Public events

    Place each accessible unit on firm, level ground along a route guests can actually use.

  • Construction projects

    Keep accessible units near the work area so a long walk is not part of the plan.

  • Community gatherings

    Put one in every cluster of units, not just one for the whole site.

  • Accessible venue plans

    Check the venue's accessibility plan and any permit conditions before booking.

ADA Portable Toilet Rental FAQs

Clear answers for planning quantities, placement, service, delivery, and pricing.

Accessible portable restrooms typically provide ground-level entry, a wider doorway, more interior maneuvering space, support rails, and accessible door hardware. Available models can vary.

Where portable toilets are clustered, the 2010 ADA Standards require at least 5%, and no fewer than one, to be accessible in each cluster. The calculator counts accessible units per restroom location. Venue and local rules can add requirements, so confirm the plan with the authority having jurisdiction.
