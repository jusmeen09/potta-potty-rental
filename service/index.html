Compare rental options

Most sites use a mix. Pick per zone based on who uses the area, how long they are there, and what the site can support.

  • Standard porta potty

    What it is
    One cabin over a sealed holding tank, with a vent, a self-closing lockable door and a paper dispenser.
    Needs
    Firm, level ground and a truck route. No power or water.
    One day
    About $75 to $200 per unit in published guides
    On your call sheet
    0

  • ADA portable toilet

    What it is
    A larger cabin with ground-level entry, a wider door, grab rails and room to turn a wheelchair.
    Needs
    Firm, level ground and an approach route a wheelchair can use.
    How many
    At least 5% of clustered units, and never fewer than one per cluster (2010 ADA Standards).
    One day
    About $110 to $225 per unit in published guides
    On your call sheet
    0

  • Restroom trailer

    What it is
    Flushing toilets, running-water sinks and interior lighting. Many models add climate control.
    Needs
    A power connection, a water supply or filled tank, a level pad and room to tow and set up.
    One day
    About $300 to $2,500+ in a Northeast vendor's guide; about $900 to $3,000 a weekend nationally
    On your call sheet
    0

  • Handwashing station

    What it is
    A fresh-water tank, foot pumps, basins and soap, with the used water held in a separate tank.
    Needs
    No plumbing. A spot beside the toilets or the food area.
    Price
    About $25 to $40 a day from one vendor; about $155 a week nationally
