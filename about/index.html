Our story

Portable restrooms without the runaround.

Star is a rental inquiry and referral website. We do not own portable toilets, operate delivery trucks, or perform cleaning and pumping.

Customers can use the planning guides and contact details to request rental information. Inquiries may be sold or shared with local rental vendors so they can respond.

The vendor you choose confirms availability, equipment, price, delivery, servicing, and pickup. A request through Star is not a confirmed booking or a guarantee that a provider serves the address.
