Terms of Service

Simple terms for a call-first rental service.

Rental inquiry service

Star Portable Restrooms publishes planning information and collects rental inquiries. Star does not own or service equipment. Requests may be passed or sold to local vendors who can respond about portable toilet, restroom trailer, or handwashing rentals.

Quotes and bookings

An inquiry is not a confirmed booking. The provider sets the equipment, availability, price, billing period, delivery, service, pickup, and cancellation terms. Confirm these in writing with that provider before ordering.

Planning information

Published price examples are third-party regional estimates, not Star offers. Images show representative equipment. Model specifications and local requirements must be checked for the actual site and proposed equipment.

Sharing your inquiry

Read our privacy policy for how contact and project details may be shared with vendors. Ask before providing details if you need clarification.

Active rentals

For equipment, servicing, delivery, billing, or pickup issues, contact the provider named on your rental agreement.

Contact

For questions about Star’s inquiry service, call +1 (833) 920-1299 or email hello@starportablerestrooms.com.
