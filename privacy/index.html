Privacy Policy

This page explains the information received through rental inquiries and how it may be shared with providers.

What we collect

When you call +1 (833) 920-1299 or email hello@starportablerestrooms.com, we receive your contact details and rental information (delivery ZIP code, dates, project type) to help a provider assess and respond to your request.

Browsing analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity and Cloudflare analytics to understand use of the site. These services may process browser, device, network, interaction, and session information using their tracking technologies. Avoid sending sensitive information that is not needed for a rental inquiry.

How we use it

Star operates a rental inquiry and referral service. We may sell or share your inquiry, contact details, and project information with local rental vendors so they can contact you about the request. Those vendors set their own prices, availability, and rental terms. We also use service providers for site operation and analytics.

Your inquiry and privacy requests

Before sharing your details, you can ask how your request will be handled. Contact us to ask about your information, object to further referral contact, or request correction or deletion. Do not provide payment information through this website; arrange the rental directly with the selected vendor.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Call +1 (833) 920-1299 (Mon–Sat 7am–7pm, Sun 7am–12pm CST) or email hello@starportablerestrooms.com.
