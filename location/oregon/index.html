Job-site compliance

Construction Sanitation Rules in Oregon

Oregon runs its own OSHA-approved State Plan, so construction sanitation on private job sites is enforced by Oregon OSHA rather than federal OSHA. A State Plan must be at least as effective as the federal standard and is permitted to be stricter, so check the Oregon OSHA rule directly rather than assuming the federal table is the whole picture.

The federal baseline it has to match is 29 CFR 1926.51(c)(1): one toilet facility for 20 or fewer workers, then one toilet seat and one urinal per 40 workers, and per 50 workers once a crew passes 200.

Accessibility is federal everywhere: where portable toilets are clustered, the 2010 ADA Standards require at least 5% and never fewer than one accessible unit per cluster. Local permit conditions in Salem and other Oregon jurisdictions may add requirements on top.
