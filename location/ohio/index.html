Construction Sanitation Rules in Ohio

Private-sector construction in Ohio falls under federal OSHA jurisdiction, so 29 CFR 1926.51(c)(1) Table D-1 applies directly: one toilet facility for 20 or fewer workers, then one toilet seat and one urinal per 40 workers, and per 50 workers once a crew passes 200.

That table is a legal minimum rather than a comfort target. Crews spread across a large Ohio site often justify more units than the count alone suggests, simply because walking time is real money.

Accessibility is federal everywhere: where portable toilets are clustered, the 2010 ADA Standards require at least 5% and never fewer than one accessible unit per cluster. Local permit conditions in Columbus and other Ohio jurisdictions may add requirements on top.
